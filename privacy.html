Contact form and document registration

When you use the contact form or register to download the peer document, we receive the data you enter: first name, last name and work email, and, if you choose to give them, country, company, role, programme stage, phone and message.

We use this data to reply to your request, to send you the document, and to follow up on that request. We do not sell it and do not add you to a newsletter.

The forms are processed by Formspree, Inc. (USA), which forwards each submission to us by email and stores it in its account dashboard. Data may therefore be transferred to the USA. Formspree acts as our processor.

We keep form data as long as needed for the request and any business relationship that follows, and delete it on request.

Booking a call

If you book a call through the calendar link, the booking is handled by Microsoft Bookings, part of Microsoft 365, operated by Microsoft. Microsoft processes your name, email address, the chosen time and any notes you add, and sends the meeting invitation. Microsoft acts as our processor.

Hosting and statistics

The website is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP address, browser type and time of access to deliver the site and protect it against attacks.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, pages viewed, countries and referring sites. It sets no cookies and does not track you across websites.

The site stores one entry in your browser's local storage when you close the contact pop-up, so it is not shown again for 30 days. It contains only the date of closing.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google servers to load them, which transmits your IP address to Google.
